Americano
=========
Also known as: Caffè Americano
Origin: Italy/WWII
Brew Method: Dilute espresso with hot water.
Ratio: 1 shot + 90–120 ml hot water
Roast: Any
Water Temperature: 90–96°C
Caffeine: ≈63–126 mg (1–2 shots)
Flavor Notes: smooth, lighter body, toasty

Overview:
Americano softens espresso’s intensity while preserving its aromatics through dilution.

FAQs:
- Q: Is Americano the same as brewed coffee?
  A: No; base is espresso plus water, so flavor and body differ from drip.
