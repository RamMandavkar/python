Cold Brew
=========
Also known as: Toddy
Origin: United States/Japan (iced methods)
Brew Method: Immersion in cold water for 12–18 hours, then filter.
Ratio: 1:5 to 1:8 concentrate; dilute to taste
Roast: Medium–dark
Water Temperature: Room temp / refrigerated
Caffeine: Varies (often higher per serving)
Flavor Notes: low acidity, smooth, chocolate, nutty

Overview:
Cold brew yields a smooth, low-acidity beverage that can be served over ice or with milk.

FAQs:
- Q: Why is Cold Brew less acidic?
  A: Cold extraction limits acid solubility compared to hot brewing.
