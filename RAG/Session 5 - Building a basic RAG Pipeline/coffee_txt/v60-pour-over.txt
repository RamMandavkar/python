V60 Pour-Over
=============
Also known as: Hario V60
Origin: Japan
Brew Method: Conical dripper; pulse pours with bloom phase.
Ratio: 1:15 to 1:17 (e.g., 15g → 250 ml)
Roast: Light–medium
Water Temperature: 92–96°C
Caffeine: ≈80–120 mg per 240 ml
Flavor Notes: clean, bright, floral/fruit-forward

Overview:
V60 highlights clarity and aroma characteristics of lighter roasts and precise pour control.

FAQs:
- Q: Why does V60 taste cleaner?
  A: Paper filters trap oils and fines, emphasizing clarity and acidity.
