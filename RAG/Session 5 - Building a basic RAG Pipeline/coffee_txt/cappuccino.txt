Cappuccino
==========
Also known as: Cap
Origin: Italy
Brew Method: Espresso with equal parts steamed milk and foam.
Ratio: 1:1:1 (espresso : milk : foam)
Roast: Medium
Water Temperature: 90–96°C
Caffeine: ≈63–126 mg
Flavor Notes: balanced, frothy, cocoa

Overview:
Cappuccino balances espresso strength with airy foam and a smaller milk volume.

FAQs:
- Q: How is Cappuccino different from Latte?
  A: More foam and less milk, so stronger coffee flavor and airy texture.
