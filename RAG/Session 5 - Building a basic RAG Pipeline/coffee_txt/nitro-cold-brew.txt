Nitro Cold Brew
===============
Also known as: Nitro
Origin: United States
Brew Method: Cold-brew concentrate infused with nitrogen and served on tap.
Ratio: Concentrate diluted 1:1–1:2 before nitro
Roast: Medium–dark
Water Temperature: Cold
Caffeine: Varies (often higher)
Flavor Notes: creamy mouthfeel, smooth, stout-like

Overview:
Nitrogen infusion creates a creamy, cascading head and a stout-like mouthfeel.

FAQs:
- Q: Does Nitro add calories?
  A: Nitrogen adds texture, not calories; toppings/syrups do add calories.
