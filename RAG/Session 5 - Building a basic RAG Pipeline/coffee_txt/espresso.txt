Espresso
========
Also known as: Short Black
Origin: Italy
Brew Method: High-pressure extraction (~9 bar) for ~25–30 seconds.
Ratio: 1:2 (18g coffee → 36g yield)
Roast: Medium–dark
Water Temperature: 92–96°C
Caffeine: ≈63 mg per shot (30 ml)
Flavor Notes: intense, syrupy, caramel, dark chocolate

Overview:
Espresso is a concentrated coffee served in small shots, prized for its syrupy body and crema.

FAQs:
- Q: Is Espresso strong?
  A: Perceived strength is high; actual caffeine depends on dose and shot length.
- Q: Best roast for Espresso?
  A: Medium to medium-dark roasts highlight body and sweetness.
