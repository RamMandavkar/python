Flat White
==========
Also known as: FW
Origin: Australia/New Zealand
Brew Method: Espresso with velvety microfoam milk, thin foam layer.
Ratio: Double shot + 120–160 ml milk
Roast: Light–medium
Water Temperature: 90–96°C
Caffeine: ≈126 mg (double shot)
Flavor Notes: silky, coffee-forward, balanced

Overview:
The flat white marries a double espresso with fine microfoam for a richer coffee presence.

FAQs:
- Q: Flat White vs Cappuccino?
  A: Flatter microfoam, less foam volume, more coffee-forward profile.
