Espresso Macchiato
==================
Also known as: Macchiato
Origin: Italy
Brew Method: Espresso ‘stained’ with a dollop of milk foam.
Ratio: Single shot + 1–2 tsp foam
Roast: Medium–dark
Water Temperature: 92–96°C
Caffeine: ≈63 mg
Flavor Notes: bold, slightly creamy

Overview:
A macchiato keeps espresso intensity but softens the edge with a touch of foam.

FAQs:
- Q: Is Macchiato sweet?
  A: Traditional macchiato is not sweetened; sweetness depends on milk and added syrups.
