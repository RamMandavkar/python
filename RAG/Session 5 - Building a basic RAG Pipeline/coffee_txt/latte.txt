Caffè Latte
===========
Also known as: Latte
Origin: Italy
Brew Method: Espresso with steamed milk and a thin microfoam cap.
Ratio: 1 shot + 180–240 ml milk
Roast: Medium
Water Temperature: 90–96°C
Caffeine: ≈63–126 mg (1–2 shots)
Flavor Notes: creamy, mild, sweet

Overview:
The latte emphasizes milk texture, producing a silky, mellow drink with gentle coffee notes.

FAQs:
- Q: Is Latte very sweet?
  A: Natural milk sweetness dominates; added syrups increase sweetness.
