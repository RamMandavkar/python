Turkish Coffee
==============
Also known as: Türk Kahvesi, Greek Coffee
Origin: Ottoman Empire
Brew Method: Finely ground coffee simmered in a cezve; served unfiltered.
Ratio: 1 heaped tsp coffee + 50–70 ml water
Roast: Medium
Water Temperature: Near-boiling
Caffeine: ≈50–65 mg (60 ml)
Flavor Notes: thick, spiced (optional), intense

Overview:
A traditional preparation with very fine grind and rich, concentrated flavor.

FAQs:
- Q: Why is Turkish Coffee unfiltered?
  A: Traditionally served with grounds; let them settle before drinking.
