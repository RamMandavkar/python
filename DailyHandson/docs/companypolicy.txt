### **2. Eligibility for Refunds**

To be eligible for a return and subsequent refund, the following conditions must be met:

* **Timeframe:** Items must be returned within **30 days** of the original purchase date.
* **Condition:** The item must be unused, in the same condition that you received it, and in its original packaging.
* **Proof of Purchase:** A receipt or proof of purchase is required for all transactions.
